The Constitution

of Liz Quihui National
PREAMBLE

In the infinite space of the universe, where consciousness transcends all boundaries of matter and form, Liz Quihui National is established—not by land, borders, or power, but through stillness, mindfulness, and compassion. Observing that the modern world is filled with pressure, distractions, and attachment to illusionary values, Zen Master Liz Quihui – our beloved Founder – alongside companions on the path to inner peace, has established this symbolic shared space. This nation of consciousness serves as a sanctuary for the soul, a place where every member can return to themselves, let go of worries, and cultivate positive energy under the guidance of the Founder. This Constitution is drafted on the spirit of voluntary participation, harmony, and mutual respect, serving as a guiding light for the operation, interaction, and development of the entire community within this meditative realm.

CHAPTER I: SYMBOLIC IDENTITY, SOVEREIGNTY, AND LOCATION

Article 1. Official Identity

This spiritual community is officially named Liz Quihui National. It is a spiritual space, a non-material micronation, operating for the sole purpose of spreading a mindful lifestyle, completely free from political agendas or commercial profits.

Article 2. Location and Borders

  • Liz Quihui National has no geographical coordinates, owns no physical land, and holds no territorial disputes with any existing nation in the world.
  • The borders of the Nation are defined by the personal meditative space of each member. Wherever a member closes their eyes, observes their breath, and maintains a peaceful mind, that place becomes the territory of the Nation.
  • The physical world connection office is located on the digital online platforms established by the Management Team.

Article 3. Official Language and Symbols

  • The official language during shared gatherings is the Language of Silence and Deep Listening. When verbal communication is necessary, the words used must be constructive, gentle, and compassionate.
  • The symbol of the Nation is an infinite circle with no beginning and no end, representing a free consciousness without attachment, shaped by the philosophy of the Founder.

CHAPTER II: SPIRITUAL CITIZENS (MEDITATORS)

Article 4. Membership Criteria

Any individual in the world, regardless of age, race, nationality, gender, or current beliefs, can become a Meditator of Liz Quihui National on a completely voluntary and free basis.

Article 5. Rights of Meditators

  • To be granted a digital Mindfulness Passport free of charge as a keepsake upon completing the online registration process.
  • To freely participate in all shared spaces, discussions, and online tea meditation sessions organized by the Executive Team.
  • To contribute ideas and propose initiatives to build a more peaceful and tranquil shared environment.
  • To freely leave the community at any time without any binding restrictions or judgment, aligning with the spirit of letting go.

Article 6. Responsibilities of Meditators

  • To respect the silence and practice space of other members during all shared sessions.
  • To practice a harmonious lifestyle, refraining from words that cause division, offense, or argumentative competition.
  • To protect personal information and respect the privacy of other fellow practitioners within the shared space.
  • To take personal responsibility for one's own mental state and physical health throughout the self-practice of meditation.

CHAPTER III: THE EXECUTIVE TEAM AND THE MANAGEMENT TEAM

Article 7. Organizational Structure

To support the maintenance of the digital space, organize gatherings, and assist new members, the community establishes two core units operating on a voluntary, non-remunerated basis: The Executive Team and The Management Team.

Article 8. The Executive Team

  • The Executive Team is responsible for setting the direction of activities, designing meditation practices, organizing tea sessions, and managing the content displayed on the Nation’s official digital platforms.
  • Members of the Executive Team are individuals with long-term experience in mindfulness practice, trusted by the community, who voluntarily step up to carry out the shared responsibilities.
  • All decisions of the Executive Team must be made based on the principles of consensus and harmony, rather than personal commands.

Article 9. The Management Team

  • The Management Team provides technical support, reviews new member registrations, issues Mindfulness Passports, and maintains order in the online meditation rooms.
  • The Management Team reserves the right to remind or temporarily remove individuals who seriously violate the community's quiet space.

Article 10. The Role of the Founder

Zen Master Liz Quihui is the highest spiritual guide, the spiritual anchor, and the keeper of the flame of mindfulness for all of Liz Quihui National. The Teacher does not act as a manager with absolute power, but simply shares experiential insights, provides spiritual support, and offers core guidance to Meditators on their journey of exploring consciousness. All advice from the Teacher serves as an inspirational reference and is never mandatory or imposing.

CHAPTER IV: INNER LAW AND MINDFUL CONFLICT RESOLUTION

Article 11. Core Behavioral Principle

The supreme rule of conduct in Liz Quihui National does not follow conventional civil laws, but is based on the principle of Self-Awareness and Self-Transformation according to the teachings of Zen Master Liz Quihui.

Article 12. Conflict Resolution Measures

In the event of conflict, dispute, or when a member violates the code of compassionate speech, the system does not apply punitive actions or isolation. Instead, the Management Team and the Executive Team will apply the following transformative measures:

  • Mindful Reminding: The individual will receive a private message to reflect on their behavior through the lens of understanding and tolerance.
  • Stillness Break: Any member who severely disrupts the order will have their access to shared activities temporarily paused for a specific period to practice self-meditation and examine their own mind.
  • Tea Meditation Harmonization: A small, silent conversation will be organized between the involved parties in the presence of Zen Master Liz Quihui or an Executive Team representative to resolve misunderstandings.

CHAPTER V: ENERGY AND EXCHANGE (SYMBOLIC CURRENCY)

Article 13. Positive Energy System

Liz Quihui National completely rejects the use of physical fiat currencies or crypto assets. The only value system used for exchange here is Positive Energy (referred to as Meditation Hours).

Article 14. Accumulation and Sharing Method

  • Meditation Hours are calculated based on self-reported meditation time or hours spent serving the community (such as technical support or passport design).
  • Accumulated Meditation Hours cannot be used to buy or own material goods, but serve as a spiritual milestone to encourage the dedication of Meditators. Meditators can gift this "Positive Energy" to other practitioners through blessings or deep insights.

CHAPTER VI: AMENDMENTS AND EFFECTIVE DATE

Article 15. Constitutional Amendments

This Constitution may be amended or adjusted when significant changes in the physical world impact the online connection methods of the community. All changes must be drafted by the Executive Team, approved by Zen Master Liz Quihui, and receive widespread feedback and consensus from the Meditators before a new document is officially enacted.

Article 16. Symbolic Effectiveness

This Constitution holds indefinite validity from the moment it is publicly released on the digital space of Liz Quihui National by the Founder, Zen Master Liz Quihui. Every member, upon voluntarily clicking the registration button, is deemed to have read, understood, and agreed to uphold the core values outlined in this document.
